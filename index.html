质量管理QUALITY

尺寸、刻线、表面
逐项核查Quality control throughout
production

围绕尺寸、刻度、字符及表面状态，对关键质量要求进行核查。Critical dimensions, graduations, markings and surface condition are checked against the agreed requirements.

01

技术文件确认Technical review

核对材质、尺寸、公差、孔位、刻度及标识内容。Material, dimensions, tolerances, mounting details, graduations and markings are reviewed.

02

过程检验In-process inspection

对关键尺寸、刻线字符及表面状态进行检验。Critical dimensions, engraved characters and surface condition are inspected.

03

出厂核查Final verification

对产品外观、数量和标识信息进行核查。Appearance, quantity and identification details are verified before dispatch.
